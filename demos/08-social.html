NF @neon_witch · posted 2h ago · #frameworks

Why I built PixelForge (and why I kept it classless)

I tried NES.css. It was beautiful, but every button required class="nes-btn is-primary". That's three classes for one button. By the time I had a 50-line form, my markup was unreadable. So I built PixelForge. Same aesthetic, zero classes, semantic HTML only.
